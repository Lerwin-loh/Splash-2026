How To Use This App

  1. Upload the latest raw registration export in Step 1. Do not upload a previously generated allocation workbook there.
  2. If you are continuing from an earlier batch, open Step 2 and upload the previous generated allocation workbook.
  3. In Step 2, choose the previous worksheet that contains the allocated applicant rows. The app will show previous group and subdomain counts.
  4. In Step 3, confirm the three preference columns for 1st, 2nd, and 3rd option.
  5. In Step 4, set the maximum applicant limit and mentor counts for each subdomain.
  6. Check that the mentor totals match and that the app says it is ready to generate.
  7. Click Generate Allocation in Step 5.
  8. Review the Results Dashboard, then download the allocated Excel file.
  9. For the next batch, use the downloaded allocated Excel file as the previous allocation workbook in Step 2.

How Allocation Works

  1. Applicants are placed alternately into Group 1 and Group 2 according to registration order.
  2. Group 1 attends the panel discussion first, while Group 2 attends mentoring first.
  3. The two groups then switch activities.
  4. Each applicant is assigned to two different mentoring subdomains.
  5. The application first attempts to provide the two highest-ranked available choices.
  6. When a preferred subdomain is full, another ranked choice or available subdomain is used.
  7. Mentor numbers are used only to control the total capacity of each subdomain.
  8. Individual mentor assignments are not created.
  9. Mentor capacity is reused because the two groups attend mentoring at different times.
  10. The participant limit per mentor automatically increases only when necessary.
  11. When continuing from a previous allocation, matched existing allocation fields are copied over unchanged.
